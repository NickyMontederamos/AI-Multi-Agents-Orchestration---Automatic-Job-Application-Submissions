# Archetype: SOC Analyst

**Lead with:** the home lab and hands-on security operations work — this is the
strongest real evidence for an L1 SOC role.

**Summary framing:** IT professional building toward SOC/security-operations work
through a self-built home lab, structured TryHackMe practice, and a real workplace
phishing-triage exercise — not just certifications on paper.

**Skills to prioritize:**
- SIEM tooling: Splunk Enterprise v9.x (self-built home lab)
- Home lab architecture: 4 VMs across 2 isolated networks (Monitoring Network with
  Windows 10 target, Windows Server 2022 AD domain controller, Ubuntu Server +
  Splunk Enterprise; isolated Attack Network with Kali Linux)
- SOC simulation exercises: brute-force attacks, lateral movement, AD enumeration,
  Sysmon telemetry analysis
- Email header forensics: SPF/DKIM/DMARC analysis, Cofense ML score interpretation
  (applied in a real workplace phishing triage exercise)
- TryHackMe Hacker rank, PH monthly rank #15, 47 rooms completed (SOC L1 and Cyber
  Defense paths), 16 badges
- PCI DSS compliance handling, fraud escalation, secure card workflows (from BPO
  experience — relevant to incident-handling discipline under pressure)

**Experience to lead with:** Independent Portfolio Projects (home lab), Volunteer
Cybersecurity Awareness Speaker, BPO fraud-escalation role.

**Certifications to lead with:** Google Cybersecurity Certificate series, Cisco
Introduction to Cybersecurity.

**Likely gaps for this archetype:** no formal SOC employment history yet; no
XSOAR/SOAR platform experience; TryHackMe and home-lab work is real but self-directed,
not employer-verified — name this plainly rather than implying employed SOC tenure.
